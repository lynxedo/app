📻 Radio — Phase 0

Does recycling the recorder every few seconds clip a syllable on this phone?

Two different tests — do both
  1. Read a paragraph out loud, naturally, for 20 seconds. Play it back. This is the test for choppiness — whether the joins interrupt the flow of a sentence.
  2. Count steadily — “one, two, three…” to twenty. Play it back. This is the test for loss — a missing number is unmissable, where a missing fraction of a second inside a sentence isn't.

They answer different questions and a run can pass one and fail the other. Counting has natural gaps between the numbers, so it hides choppiness; connected prose hides a small loss. Do both, then Copy results.

This round finds out whether the chop is in the recording or in the playing. Do a Continuous take, then tap 🔇 Release microphone before you play anything back. Then play the take both ways: Web Audio and native. Then record a control — a plain, uncut recording — release the mic again, and play that both ways. Four listens. Tell me which were choppy.

If the control is choppy, nothing about pieces matters — it's how this phone records or plays through a web page. If everything is clean through native and choppy through Web Audio, the recording is fine and the fault is the speaker path, which I can route around. The log records which capture method ran and whether voice processing was on.

The mic deliberately stays open between takes — holding it open across piece boundaries is the thing being tested. Release it when you're done so the recording dot goes away.

Settings

The PRD's proposal is 2000 then 5000. Try 5000/5000 too — more boundaries per sentence makes a join easier to hear.

Auto measures the silence the encoder put at the front of each piece and skips exactly that much, never more than the cap. Fixed skips the same amount off every piece. The cap matters: without it, auto would also eat a real pause in your speech and run your words together.

The phone's own clean-up of the microphone. Normally wanted — but noise suppression can eat quiet consonants and auto-gain can pump, and either can read as "choppy". Turning it off reopens the mic on your next hold.

Recycle recorder stops and restarts the recorder at every join — simple, but every platform we've tried drops a piece of a word each time. Continuous capture never stops listening: it takes the raw sound straight off the microphone and cuts the pieces itself, so a join cannot lose anything — there is nothing to stop and restart. The cost is size, and you'll see it in the KB column.

The iPhone only offers one, so there's nothing to choose there. On a desktop or Android this is worth switching — it tells us whether a bad result is the chopping technique or just that browser being slow at that particular format. Changing it starts a fresh take.
